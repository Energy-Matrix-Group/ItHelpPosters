Putting them up

Where each one goes, and how to print it

PosterPaperWhere it goesColour
Print it

Print at 100% (Actual size) with the margins at None. Office printers leave a thin white edge; that's expected.

Save ink

Choose White under Colour. The posters keep coral for the highlights and print well in black and white too.

Test the code

Before a poster goes up, scan its QR code with your own phone and check the ticket opens for the right room.

Make it last

Laminate the posters, or slip them into a frame. Put the table tent in a stand or laminate it before folding.

How the QR codes work

From the room to a ticket

The code goes through the EMG Portal, so it works on any phone and whatever Ticketing's own address is. Someone who isn't signed in signs in with their work email first, and lands back on the form.

  1. ScanThe phone's camera reads the code on the poster.
  2. Sign inThe portal checks who it is, and that they can use Ticketing.
  3. New TicketTicketing opens New Ticket with the room and the problem filled in.
  4. Create TicketThey add what happened and send it. IT sees which room it came from.
